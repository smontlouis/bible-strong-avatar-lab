Two clip tracks: `opacity` fades the whole avatar (spinner marks included), and `eyeSize` scales both eyes about their place, on every face. Both rest at 1, are joined and counted in the loop's rest pose. `CharacterScene.opacity` carries the avatar's opacity; the three renderers give it to the moved group in place of the spinner's own.
