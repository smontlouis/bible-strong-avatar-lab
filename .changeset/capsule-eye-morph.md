Eyes pass from one face to another through a capsule: its middle, angle, length and width are mixed. Straight eyes (`neutral`, `surprised`, `sad`, `squinted`, `inspecting`) turn about their middle the short way instead of shrinking through it; towards or away from the oval of `calm`, the capsule has the oval's size and swells into it late on the way there, instead of shrinking to a dot. Bent eyes (`happy`, `confused`, `sleepy`) still mix their numbers. New exports: `morphEyeShapes`, `eyeMorphOf`, the `EyeMorph` type, and `FaceEye.ovalMix`; `mixEyeShapes` is unchanged.
