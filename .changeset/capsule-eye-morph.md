Eyes towards or away from an oval face (`calm`) pass through a capsule of the oval's size instead of shrinking to a dot: its middle, angle, length and width are mixed, and it swells into the oval late on the way there. Bent eyes (`happy`, `confused`, `sleepy`) still mix their numbers. New exports: `morphEyeShapes`, `eyeMorphOf`, the `EyeMorph` type, and `FaceEye.ovalMix`; `mixEyeShapes` is unchanged.
